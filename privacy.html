Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Who uses this app

Comedy Vault Clips is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It has no other users.

What data it accesses

When the owner connects an account, the app receives an access token from YouTube (Google) or TikTok and basic profile information (account name/ID) needed to upload videos. Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are used solely to upload the owner's clips and check their upload status.

What it does not do

It does not collect data about viewers or other users, does not sell or share any data, does not use data for advertising, and does not store data on any server.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google account (Security → Third-party access) or TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions), and delete the local token files.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
